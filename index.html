About

SEO specialist who writes code.

I'm an in-house technical SEO and digital marketing specialist with 18+ months growing organic visibility for two sister engineering-outsourcing firms in mechanical design and BIM/CAD. I own technical SEO, content and link acquisition end to end: audits, keyword and competitor research, guest-post outreach, and reporting in Google Analytics 4 and Search Console.

Along the way I noticed how much of the job was repetitive, so I started automating it. I've built a suite of Python tools (scrapers, auditors and AI content generators) that took on-page analysis from 1–2 days down to a single day per site, roughly halving audit turnaround. I use them daily on live SEO work.

Today I focus on where SEO and AI meet: n8n workflows, AI-assisted research and content, and small web tools that solve real problems.
